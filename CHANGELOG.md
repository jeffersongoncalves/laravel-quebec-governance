# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-18

First release of laravel-quebec-governance: the person in charge of the protection of personal information (Quebec Law 25, s. 3.1), governance policies (s. 3.2), an inventory of processing activities joined with the declared personal information, a gap check command, and privacy policy and person in charge Blade components in English and French. Part of the Quebec Law 25 suite for Laravel.
